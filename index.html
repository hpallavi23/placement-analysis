Evidence-Aware Scoring: Reported skills carry an evidence-based weighting factor (λ).
E0 No claim recorded (λ = 0.0) E1 Self-reported skill without approved evidence (λ = 0.5) E2 Verified with ≥1 approved evidence submission (λ = 1.0)
Formula: Adjusted Proficiency = Reported × λ. Click “Evidence” on any skill to submit proof or view review status.
